01

Overview

Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins (《灵墟纪》) is an offline-first, single-player Eastern ink cultivation simulation for iOS and modern Web browsers. It requires no user account, no email address, and connects to no external tracking servers.

We believe an authentic cultivation experience requires tranquility and deep privacy. We do not collect personal information, gameplay telemetry, analytics, advertising identifiers (IDFA), precise location, or biometric data from the application.

IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

No user accounts. No analytics tracking. No advertising SDKs. No telemetry. Zero personal data collected.

02

Information Stored on Your Device

To deliver persistent offline gameplay, Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins saves your cultivation progress locally on your device:

  • Cultivation State: Current realm tier (Qi Refinement through Void Tribulation), gathered spiritual Qi, insight, and vitality points.
  • Inventory & Crafting: Spiritual stones, forged blades, alchemy herbs, pills, and inscribed talismans.
  • World Progress: Explored regions (Qingya Peak, Lan Sea, Northern Wastes, Yunmeng Marsh, Ethereal Ruins), sect reputation, and completed story arcs.
  • Preferences: Sound toggle, selected locale / language, and notification permission states.

This data is stored strictly in the app’s sandboxed local container on iOS (or HTML5 localStorage in the web version). We cannot access, retrieve, or inspect this information. It is never transmitted across the network.

03

Local Notifications

With your explicit permission, the app schedules local notifications to alert you when an in-game meditation session concludes, when a sect mission is ready for claim, or when a breakthrough tribulation opportunity arises.

These notifications are computed and scheduled entirely on your local device using Apple’s local notification framework. The app does not utilize remote Apple Push Notification servers (APNs), and no gameplay or device data is ever transmitted to external notification relays.

You can grant, modify, or revoke notification permissions at any time within iOS Settings > Notifications > 灵墟纪 (Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins).

04

Audio & Web Audio API

The game utilizes synthesized Web Audio and native procedural audio generation (such as Guqin plucks, bronze bells, sword slashes, and thunder claps). The game does not access your microphone, does not record ambient sound, and respects your device’s hardware Silent Mode switch.

05

Third Parties & External SDKs

Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins contains no third-party marketing frameworks, no advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no external telemetry services.

The game is completely ad-free and contains no in-app purchases or pay-to-win mechanisms.

06

This Website

This marketing website (hosted on GitHub Pages) is a collection of static HTML, CSS, and asset files. It sets no tracking cookies, uses no Google Analytics, and requires no login.

Standard web server access logs may be generated by GitHub Inc. for security and infrastructure reliability in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.

07

Your Control & Data Deletion

Because all game progress resides exclusively on your local device:

  • In-App Reset: You can wipe your save data at any time from the in-game Settings sheet by choosing "Reincarnate / Reset Save".
  • Uninstalling the App: Deleting the app from your iOS device permanently purges all sandboxed save files, preferences, and local notifications in accordance with standard iOS behavior.

Because we do not maintain a cloud database or user registry, there is no remote copy of your save data to delete or recover.

08

Children’s Privacy

The game does not collect personal identifiable information from any player, including children under the age of 13. It is compliant with COPPA and international privacy frameworks.

09

Changes to This Policy

Should future updates introduce cloud synchronization or optional online community features, this privacy policy will be updated prior to any data collection, and notice will be presented within the application.
