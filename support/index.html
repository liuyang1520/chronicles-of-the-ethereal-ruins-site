DAOIST COMPENDIUM

Frequently asked questions

01 How do I begin cultivation and break through?

When you first awaken in your mountain sanctum, select Meditate (入定修炼) to begin gathering spiritual Qi. Cultivation progresses in real time, even while the game is closed.

Once your Qi reservoir reaches the required threshold, return to your sanctum and choose Break Through (尝试破境). Weathering the heavenly tribulation advances your realm (e.g. from Qi Refinement to Foundation Establishment) and unlocks new mystic techniques and regions.

02 Why am I not receiving cultivation notifications?

Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins schedules notifications strictly on your local device. To ensure alerts arrive reliably:

  1. Open iOS Settings, scroll down to 灵墟纪 (or Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins), and select Notifications.
  2. Ensure Allow Notifications is toggled on, along with Banner and Lock Screen alerts.
  3. Inside the game, open the Settings sheet (gear icon) and verify that Completion Alerts are enabled.
03 Does the game require an internet connection?

No. Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins is an offline-first experience. All simulation algorithms, story branches, artisan recipes, 3D relic visualizers, and procedural audio run entirely locally on your device. You can cultivate atop real mountain summits or in airplane mode without any loss of functionality.

04 Which languages are supported?

The game provides verified localization across 6 major languages:

  • English: Chronicles of the Ethereal Ruins
  • Simplified Chinese (简体中文): 灵墟纪
  • Traditional Chinese (繁體中文): 靈墟紀
  • Japanese (日本語): 霊墟紀 (れいきょき)
  • Korean (한국어): 영허기
  • Spanish (Español): Crónicas de las Ruinas Etéreas

You can switch your preferred language at any time in the in-game Settings sheet.

05 How does the sound and audio engine work?

The game features a procedural Web Audio acoustic synthesizer reproducing ancient Guqin string plucks, bronze bells, sword strikes, and ambient rainfall without requiring large audio downloads.

The audio engine respects your device's hardware Silent Mode switch. If you hear no sound, confirm your device is unmuted, or toggle the Sound option in the in-game Settings menu.

06 How do I forge blades and craft alchemy pills?

After reaching Qi Refinement Stage 4, you can visit the Sect tab. The Alchemy Chamber allows you to combine spiritual herbs (such as Dew Grass and Moon Petals) into restorative pills.

At the Sword Terrace, you can refine spiritual ore (such as Red Smelt Copper) into personalized flying swords and talisman treasures.

07 How do I backup, restore, or erase my progress?

Save data is kept in your device's private sandboxed storage. If you perform a full iOS backup (via iCloud or Mac), your game save is included as part of standard device backup.

If you wish to reincarnate and start fresh, open the in-game Settings sheet and choose Reincarnate / Reset Save. Deleting the app from your device also wipes local save data.

08 Which iOS devices and platforms are supported?

The native iOS application supports all iPhone models running iOS 15 or later. The Web edition is fully compatible with modern versions of Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge.
